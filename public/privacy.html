Privacy

What happens to your data.

Last updated: 2026-05-21

Short version

Hudson Turansky (US-based one-person studio) collects only what's needed to deliver the thing you signed up for. No third-party advertising trackers. No data sold or shared with marketers. You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by emailing hudsonturansky@gmail.com.

The free Personalized AI Brief

When you request the free Personalized AI Brief at /ai-brief:

  • Your answers (your role, your industry, free-text descriptions of your work, the things you want to streamline, etc.) and your email address are stored in a Supabase database that only Hudson can read.
  • Your answers are sent to OpenAI's API to generate your personalized list of ideas. OpenAI's data-handling terms apply to that single API call — they don't train on API data by default.
  • Your results are stored alongside your answers so Hudson can see what the tool is producing.
  • Your email is also added to Hudson's CRM as a cold lead (status: cold; how_i_know_them: "AI use-case test"). Hudson will not add you to a newsletter, a drip campaign, or a marketing list. If Hudson contacts you, it'll be a one-off personal email — never automated.
  • One brief per email is enforced. If you want a second one, email Hudson and he'll wipe your previous submission.

Contact forms and other email submissions

When you fill out the contact form or email Hudson directly, your message and email address are stored so Hudson can read and respond. That's it.

Analytics

This site does not currently use a third-party analytics service. Server-level request logs may include IP address, user agent, and the URL you requested — kept for security and debugging only.

Cookies

The site uses a small number of strictly-necessary cookies for the optional account features (the Configurator product and the customer dashboard). No advertising cookies, no third-party tracking pixels.

Children

Not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Your rights

You can request, at any time, by emailing hudsonturansky@gmail.com:

  • A copy of any data Hudson stores about you.
  • Correction of anything that's wrong.
  • Deletion of your data (the AI Brief row, the lead record, the contact form submission — all of it).

If you're in the EU/UK, this maps roughly onto GDPR rights of access, rectification, and erasure. Hudson is the data controller.

Changes to this policy

If this policy materially changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page changes with it. There's no email list to notify you because there's no email list.
